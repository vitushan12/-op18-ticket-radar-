Ticket notifications

Get an alert when an hourly check confirms newly available OP-18 tickets. Tap an alert to open the tracker and verify with the store.

On iPhone, first add the radar to your Home Screen in Safari, then open it from that icon and return here.

Checking notification setup…

Enabling notifications registers this browser with OneSignal, our notification provider. All subscribers receive the same ticket alerts. Your local notes and favourites are not sent. Checks are approximately hourly; notifications are not instant or guaranteed.
