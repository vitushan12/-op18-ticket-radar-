Robin price alerts

Dashboard updates

The server checks Canadian retailers for Nico Robin EB05-010 alternate-art foil, targeting matching in-stock cards below CA$50 before shipping and tax.

Checks run approximately every 30 minutes and can be delayed by GitHub. The dashboard shows the check time, store coverage, current offers, and recorded price changes.

Push and email alerts are not configured for this watchlist. No automatic notification will be sent to your phone or email.

You can keep the dashboard on your iPhone Home Screen: open it in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. Installation does not enable background notifications.
